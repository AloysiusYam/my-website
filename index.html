About

I grew up in Singapore, a city that is both a global financial hub and a low-lying island facing the effects of climate change. That mix got me curious about where environmental science and money meet.

At NUS, I plan to build a solid base in environmental science and policy, and pair it with an understanding of markets. My goal is to help capital flow toward companies and projects that are good for people and the planet.
